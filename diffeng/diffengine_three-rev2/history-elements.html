The Elements

BBUs The Bi-directional Browsing Units are designed to reveal the inner workings of the mechanism. From circuit boards to gear pulleys and potentiometers - all are subject to the gaze of the museum visitor. The outer casing of the BBU is also"visible" to the internet visitor with careful observation of the web site's design motif.

Screen Savers When the BBUs are inactive, instead of the virtual museum, the monitors display screen savers inspired by the Sirens in "The Odyssey." Cyborgian women beckon viewers in the museum to directly interact with the "Engine." When a museum viewer approaches the BBU, the Sirens disappear.

Avatars Every visitor to the museum - real or virtual - has an avatar created in their image. even if the only mark of their individuality comes in the form of an identification number. Up to 45 avatars can roam the virtual museum at once.

Capture Cameras Attached to the top of the BBUs, these digital cameras are the eyes of the BBUs.

VRML The three dimensional graphic representation of the ZKM museum is made "live" by rendering it in VRML. In this way, rather than providing a static shot of the museum, visitors can navigate through the space in a hands-on or telerobotic fashion.

Ghosts Lurking within the virtual museum are "ghosts" of the past - art retrieved from Nazi Germany as well as early renditions of Babbage's Engine. These exist as moving screens within the virtual architecture.

Virtual Multi-User Server The BBUs utilize a multi-user environment, developed by Black Sun Interactive, which allows up to 45 people to interact with the exhibition simultaneously.

Telerobotics Hardware and software were custom-designed for this installation so that its physical manifestation in the ZKM Museum could be manipulated remotely.

Purgatorial Cache While purgatory is a mere passage in the life of the avatar - the middle leg of the journey - it is the first thing the museum visitor sees upon entering the museum. 15 avatars at a time suspend here on the way to their final resting place - the archive.

Web Site A dedicated site hosts information about the project's development and provides views into the real and virtual spaces. It is via this site that the virtual visitor is afforded telerobotic control over the BBU.
